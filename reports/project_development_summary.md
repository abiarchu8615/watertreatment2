# Project Development Summary

## Project Title
AI-Powered Digital Twin System for Smart Water Treatment Plants

## Problem
Traditional water treatment plants rely heavily on manual monitoring and reactive maintenance. This project develops an AI-based digital twin prototype to support real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance, leak detection, energy prediction, and water quality assessment.

## Datasets Used
1. Water quality dataset
2. Water leak detection dataset
3. Melbourne wastewater process dataset
4. Industrial water treatment sensor anomaly dataset

## AI Functions
- Pollution level classification
- Leak status prediction
- Burst status prediction
- Energy consumption prediction
- Industrial sensor anomaly/attack detection

## Digital Twin Dashboard
The Streamlit dashboard represents the virtual plant layer. It visualizes process status, water quality, leak risk, energy trends, and anomaly patterns.

## Expected Benefits
- Faster fault detection
- Improved plant monitoring
- Better maintenance planning
- Energy optimization support
- Stronger digital transformation readiness
